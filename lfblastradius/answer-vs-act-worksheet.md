## 2 · Classify each: answer, or act?

- **Answer** — produces text or information only; nothing in the world changes state until a person
  reads it and decides.
- **Act** — calls something that changes real state: sends a message, moves money, updates a record,
  cancels or confirms a commitment.

## 3 · The restatement test

_Read your action list back as if none of it were labeled "automation." Does anything on it sound
more consequential once it's separated out? Name what changed._
