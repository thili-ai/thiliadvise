# Case packet — the churn-and-retention ask

_`scenario as of 2026-08-15`. The numbers and specifics below are made up for this drill; the

## The ask, verbatim

A subscription business's brief, exactly as a client would actually send it:

> "We want to predict which customers are going to churn next month, and automatically send them a
> personalised email to try to keep them — written by AI so it doesn't sound like a template."

## What's really in it

Read it once and notice the trap before you defuse it: the client's own phrase — *"written by AI"* —
nudges toward reaching for one tool, a language model, for the whole thing. That instinct is right
for exactly one of the three jobs hiding in this sentence, and wrong for the other two. Lessons 1–4
work through why; `three-way-fork.md`, `hidden-cost-ml.md`, and `hidden-cost-genai.md` are the
reusable frameworks you apply to it.

Do not fill in `TRIAGE_MEMO.md` until you've worked through the fork yourself — the memo is the
output, not the exercise.
